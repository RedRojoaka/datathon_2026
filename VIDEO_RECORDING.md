# Record the submission video in under two minutes

A single screen recording with your voice is enough. Use the four public dashboard links in the README; leave API-key pages and private terminals closed. This is a walkthrough, not a tutorial for building the charts.

## Before recording

1. Open the four dashboards in separate browser tabs and let them load.
2. Restore the intended default filters. On Dashboard 4, start at 50% technician recovery and 5% promotion lift.
3. Rehearse the [prepared narration](royale-lab/submission/VIDEO_SCRIPT.md) once and aim for 1:50–1:55.
4. On macOS, press **Command+Shift+5**, choose **Record Selected Portion**, and frame the dashboard area. In **Options → Microphone**, select your microphone and check that narration is recorded. Make a short test recording first.

## Recording order

| Time | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Dashboard 1 headline and forecast | Fictional casino, six months of observed data, July planning, and the decision beyond replacing low earners. |
| 0:15–0:40 | Forecast validation and MAE comparison | Time-based test, $68,699 versus $218,258 daily error, and contribution after supplied costs rather than full profit. |
| 0:40–1:00 | Dashboard 2 | Change one bank filter and select one machine; explain exposure, availability, and payout variation. |
| 1:00–1:15 | Dashboard 3 | Tracked-player outcomes and the full-period 22.9% top-1% wager share; uncarded play is separate. |
| 1:15–1:45 | Dashboard 4 | $1,200 inspection, $98,800 preserved, one scenario control, unknown uplift, and how to measure results. |
| 1:45–1:55 | Saved Gemini explanation on Dashboard 4 | Identify it as a saved Gemini response grounded in evidence; all financial calculations remain in Python. |

Stop recording from the menu-bar Stop button. Play the file and confirm readable text, audible narration, no credentials, and a duration at most two minutes. If longer, shorten the narration or trim unnecessary start/end silence.

Upload the video using a method accepted by the event. An unlisted YouTube link is one option if the event accepts it; unlisted is accessible to anyone with the link. Confirm the video plays while signed out. Add the final link to the README and Devpost. Keep the video file out of the source repository.

Official references: [Apple screen-recording instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102618), [YouTube visibility settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/157177?hl=en).
